**How the SF bushing is retained.** The head carries two milled recesses: a lock-screw recess for fixed mounting
and a "slip" recess (rotate to release) for quick change (allindustrial page). One LS-0 lock screw, at R = 17/64 in
(6.75 mm) from the bushing axis (Carr Lane column R; McMaster's smallest "lock screw/bushing center-to-center" is
also 17/64 in), holds the head down. The slip fit (liner ID +2.5/+10 µm over 4.763 mm, bushing −5/0 µm) gives a
diametral clearance of 2.5–15 µm.

**Counterbore warning.** Carr Lane counterbores "extremely long small-ID bushings" per ANSI unless **-NCB** is
added (page). Order the guide bushings **-NCB** so the punch tube is guided over the full length.

## Recommendation

**Punch pair: SYNEO CR0350275N20R4 (hole, edge 0.76 mm, tube OD 0.889 mm) + SYNEO CR0650495N16R4 (OD, edge
1.397 mm, tube OD 1.651 mm)**, both 304 SS hard temper, TiN, 1.500 in (38.1 mm) straight hypodermic tubes. They are
the only catalogue tubes found that hit 0.75/1.40, and their straight shafts are what a guide bushing needs.
Washer on nominal: ⌀1.40 / ⌀0.76, wall 0.32. Ask SYNEO (RFQ) for: price, OD and edge tolerance, and whether an
**inside-edge** version of the 1.40 punch can be made (with the standard middle edge the washer is pushed into the
1.245 mm bore and needs a ⌀1.1–1.2 ejector pin; the hole slug sits in a 0.686 mm bore and is cleared with a
⌀0.6 wire). Fallback, off the shelf today: Rapid-Core 0.75 (ID 0.77, OD 1.07) + Rapid-Core 1.5 (ID 1.50, OD 1.90),
US$6.50 each at Ted Pella, with built-in ejectors — but the washer then sits at the drawing's upper OD limit
(1.50) and the exposed cannula length (what the bushing can guide) is unknown; check one in hand first.
Membrane disc: Rapid-Core 1.5 (15115-5).

**Bushing system: Carr Lane SF-12 slip/fixed renewable bushings (ordered -NCB) in one Carr Lane L-20 liner,
LS-0 lock screw** (or McMaster 8493A901 made-to-order SF if a non-standard OD/length is wanted). One liner
takes every bore from 0.34 to 2.49 mm, so the 0.889, 1.651 and 1.90 mm tubes all fit.
