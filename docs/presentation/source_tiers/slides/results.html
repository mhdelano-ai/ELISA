The band is a scenario range, not a confidence interval. Each step down is small: the bed takes 66 % of the sample instead of 8.2 %, and a valve keeps conjugate-coated walls out of the read. The project’s earlier design (dosing pump, chamber and trap) stands at nominal 0.60, median 3.5 pg/mL on a different model footing; neither is shown to be better.

Source: docs/30 §0 and results/tier_round/tiers.json. Nominal = all parameters at their best estimate. Median and range = Monte Carlo over the parameter bounds, n = 2000, seed 20261111; the 5–95 % interval is a scenario range, not a confidence interval. P = fraction of draws with a limit at or below 1 pg/mL. P at or below 10 pg/mL: 0.33, 0.54, 0.69 and 0.61. Geometry that is not published (cell gap, bore length, frit area, loop walls) is a design input and is not sampled, so the ranges are narrower than the full uncertainty. The transfer variant is at a released fraction of 0.35 (0.05–0.74), for which nothing is measured. The earlier design of the project (dosing pump, chamber and trap, docs/05) stands at nominal 0.60, median 3.5 pg/mL; its numbers and these are not on the same footing, and neither is shown to be better than the other.
