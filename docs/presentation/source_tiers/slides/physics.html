Source: docs/30 §1, facts 1–6. The curve is the kit’s conversion of a stream that passes the 3 mm electrode of the strip cell under a 0.2 mm gap (the gap is not published; 0.2 mm is assumed): 99 % at 1 µL/min, 62 % at 5 µL/min, 6.1 % at 200 µL/min. Tier 1 therefore reads at a fast flow, where product from conjugate on upstream walls is mostly not collected while the product of the enzyme on the electrode is collected in full; tiers 2 and 3 sweep their plug slowly. Capture: the electrode takes 8.2 % of the sample at 2.86 µL/min; the bed (24 mm² of bead surface) takes 66 % at 3.4 µL/min. Label loss of 25–63 % is at the best estimate of the detection antibody’s off-rate (half-life 13 min). All values are model values.
