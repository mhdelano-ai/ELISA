What went wrong on the way

Six research agents hit the usage limit and were redone as four. The kit’s first cross-check was off 2.8-fold. Neither early version of the designs survived its review.

Nine agent tasks gave results: four for research, three first reviewers and two second reviewers; the 59 findings are 35 from round 1 and 24 from round 2, each traced to a change or to an open bench question. The agent tasks were independent and all ran on the Opus model after the first failure; each wrote only its own files. The reviewers had no part in writing the designs. The calculation kit has 36 checks and a cross-check against the project’s audited model. What went wrong on the way (docs/32 §4): a workflow runs at most two agents at a time on this machine; the first six research agents stopped after about 20 min on the account’s 5-hour usage limit and left only file skeletons; the kit’s first cross-check against the audited model was off by a factor of 2.8 until a loop-plug parameter was read; the session’s worker restarted during the last revision. Revision C answers the second review but was not reviewed again (docs/31 §4.3).
