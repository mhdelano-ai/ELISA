Recommendation

Bench order: the two questions first, then tier 2

1

The two chemistry questions

Half a day, no flow system. They decide the substrate for these designs and the earlier ones.

2

Instrument facts

Two days: steady low flow, a zero-flow step, the loop switching, the cell gap, time between sampler lines.

3

Shared first experiments

Readout, free hydroquinone and conjugate sticking to beads, with the strip cell as the electrode.

4

Tier 2

Walls and frit against beads, the stop, breakthrough, bed-to-bed spread; then blanks and the limit.

5

Tier 1 beside it, if wanted

The cheapest to try: its first experiments need only strips, thiols, streptavidin and conjugate.

6

Tier 3

One valve with the bed in its loop as soon as it can be had. The transfer only after its own test.

Nothing is ordered. The round changes no gate, kill criterion or order of the bench plan; whether its experiments enter that plan is your decision.
