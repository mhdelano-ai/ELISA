The request

One request, and four answers that fixed the task

The request, 17 November

“I have the following tools at my disposal: acquity i-class bsm with sm-fl (fixed loop); Gamry 600+; 10uL flow cell that can hold any micrux screen printed electrode. Design an assay that uses these items for the absolute best LOD of human IgG. Interview me if you need input.”

The interview: four questions, four answers

What may the assay add to the set-up?

“Three different implementations with these categories.”

How much hands-on work is acceptable?

Unattended per sample; preparation at the bench beforehand is fine.

Do the limits of 100 µL injected and 60 min still apply?

Both still apply.

Which flow cell and which electrodes?

Both cells are available; use whichever gives the lower limit.

Carried over from the project: human IgG in buffer only; target 1 pg/mL; the detection limit is the mean blank plus three standard deviations of blanks, read through a calibration curve.

The request is quoted as typed. The four questions and answers are from the session record (docs/32 §2); the questions are shortened here; the first answer is quoted as typed, the other three are the options chosen. The categories of the first answer were: consumables only; passive in-line parts as well; anything. “Unattended per sample” means each determination runs without a manual step from injection to result; a fresh, pre-coated electrode or bed per determination is allowed, and no unattended queue is required (docs/26). The 100 µL limit is the injected volume; 60 min counts from injection to result. Project criteria: docs/00.
