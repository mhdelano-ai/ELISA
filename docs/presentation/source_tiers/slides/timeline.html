Source: the step tables of docs/30 §3.3, §4.3, §5.2 and §5.3, drawn from the model output. Times from injection to result: tier 1 57.1 min, tier 2 56.7, tier 3 with one valve 56.7, with the transfer 58.2. One determination is two autosampler lines (sample, conjugate); the transfer has a third (eluent). Each line is taken as 1 min before its injection (0.25–2 min in the vendor material, unverified); at 2 min per line the totals are 59.2, 58.7, 58.7 and 61.2 min. The 37–44 min of sample loading are those of tiers 1, 2 and 3 with one valve; the transfer variant loads for 25 min at 5 µL/min to make room for its 12.5 min of transfer steps. Pre-run: 12 min (tier 1), 17 min (tier 2), 10 min (tier 3). Tier 1’s reference windows are the two short rust bars on either side of its read.
