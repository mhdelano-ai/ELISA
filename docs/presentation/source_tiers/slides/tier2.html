Model, best estimate. Median of 2000 model draws: 8.4 pg/mL; nine in ten fall between 1.0 and 100. 56.7 min from injection to result.

Capture. The sample passes a bed of a million streptavidin beads at 3.4 µL/min for 37 min. The bed is packed by hand against a frit in a capillary; 66 % of the IgG is captured.

Read. Substrate in, flow stopped for 5 min, then the product is swept over a printed carbon electrode in 90 s. The electrode converts 62 % of it.

What limits it. Conjugate on the walls above the bed (45 % of the blank) and on the frit (21 %), more than on the beads (31 %).

Unproven. A zero-flow step in the pump method; the wetted area of the frit; the fit of the sleeve in the inlet port.

Source: docs/30 §4. Bed: 1×10⁶ streptavidin beads of 2.8 µm carrying the biotinylated capture fragment, packed against a frit in a fused-silica capillary of 0.20 mm bore; 19 nL, 0.6 mm long, 24 mm² of bead surface. The capillary passes through the inlet port in a sleeve and ends 1 mm above a printed carbon strip; a fresh strip with every bed. 5 nM conjugate for 4 min. Read: 215 µL of substrate at 80 µL/min, 1 min at 5 µL/min, stop 5 min, sweep 90 s at 5 µL/min; the signal is the charge of the trace minus the trace of the same stop and sweep recorded on the fresh bed and strip. Model limits for a frit of 1, 5 and 20 mm²: 1.7, 2.1 and 3.4 pg/mL. If the pump cannot stop, a continuous read at 1 µL/min gives 2.8 (optimistic: the drift over the hour is not in the kit). Fritted capillary part numbers and the sleeve fit are unverified.
