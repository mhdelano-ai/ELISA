The task after the interview

Three tiers, by what may be added to the equipment

Tier 1 · consumables only

Capture on the electrode itself

The capture antibody sits on the gold working electrode of a printed strip, and the whole sample passes over it.

Added: strips, thiols, reagents, a restrictor and a narrow transfer line.

Tier 2 · plus passive in-line parts

A bead bed at the cell inlet

A million beads packed against a frit in a capillary whose tip ends 1 mm above the electrode.

Added: the same reagents on beads, a fritted capillary, a sleeve and a fitting.

Tier 3 · anything

The same bed in the loop of a valve

One six-port valve closes the bed while product collects, and keeps conjugate-coated walls out of the read.

Added: one valve and its control from Python.

All three are the same sequential sandwich assay with the same reagents. They differ in where the IgG is captured and in what the read can exclude.

Tier definitions from the round brief (docs/26 §2) and the summary table of docs/30 §0. Tier 1 adds consumables only; tier 2 adds passive in-line parts; tier 3 may add anything. Tier 3 as designed uses one valve; a second valve and a second bed for a transfer of the immune complex are described and computed, but are conditional on a test of the transfer chemistry (see the transfer slide).
