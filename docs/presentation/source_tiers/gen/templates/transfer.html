Source: docs/30 §5.3 and §5.4; the curves are computed with the round’s kit (nominal case, released fraction varied). The transfer releases the immune complex from the capture bed and re-captures it on a clean second bed in the loop of a second valve, leaving most of the stuck conjugate behind (the non-specific label that comes along is taken as ×0.1, 0.01–0.5). The released fraction is taken as 0.35 (0.05–0.74): no release rate at 5 min is known for any of the three routes considered (a cleavable link with a hapten tag, desthiobiotin displaced by biotin, dinitrophenyl first and streptavidin second). The label then waits 17 min from the end of the conjugate step to the accumulation (about 5 min with one valve); with the unscreened detection reagent 63 % of it is gone. Pessimistic bounds: release 0.05 and half of the non-specific label coming along. The test that decides (T3-3): release in 5 min for each route, first in tubes; pass at 50 % released and a blank reduced fivefold.
