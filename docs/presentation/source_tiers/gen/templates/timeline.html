One determination is two autosampler lines (sample, conjugate); the transfer has a third (eluent). Each line is taken as 1 min before its injection (0.25–2 min in the vendor material, unverified); at 2 min per line the totals are 59.2, 58.7, 58.7 and 61.2 min. The 37–44 min of sample loading are those of tiers 1, 2 and 3 with one valve; tier 3 with transfer loads for 25 min at 5 µL/min to make room for its 12.5 min of transfer steps.
