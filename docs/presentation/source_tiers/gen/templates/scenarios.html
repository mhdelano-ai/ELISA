The credit for a F(ab′)₂ conjugate is of unverified size. “Does not decay”: the substrate’s oxidisable background stays at 10⁻⁵ of the substrate at the time of use, as the earlier model of the project assumed. 25 times more sticking and a background of 10⁻³ are the project’s scenarios S1 and S4a; the project decides on these two quantities by its G1 rule.
