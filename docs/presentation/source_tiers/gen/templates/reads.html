The read

Three ways to read the same product

Tier 1: steady current

{{art:trace_t1}}

Substrate flows at 200 µL/min. Signal: the current in a 60 s window, minus the mean of two reference windows without substrate.

Tier 2: stop, then sweep

{{art:trace_t2}}

Flow stops for 5 min and product collects around the bed. A 90 s sweep carries it over the electrode. Signal: the area of the peak.

Tier 3: closed, then opened

{{art:trace_t3}}

The valve closes the bed for 3 min while the cell stays in flow, so the peak sits on a flat baseline. Signal: its area.

In all three the potentiostat holds one electrode at the read potential from the pre-run to the end. The potential is never stepped: a step would charge about a thousand times the signal at the limit.

Sketches of the expected traces, not data. Source: docs/30 §3.1, §4.1, §5.1 and §1. Tier 1: reference window 30 s, substrate front 1.1 min, read 60 s, front back 1.1 min, reference window 30 s, all at one constant flow; the two reference windows are 3.7 min apart, and the same sequence on the fresh strip before the sample is subtracted. Tier 2: the baseline is the last 30 s of the stop; the trace of the same stop and sweep on the fresh bed and strip is subtracted, because the background steps up when fresh substrate arrives with the sweep. Tier 3: the baseline is the 30 s before the valve opens; no pre-run trace is subtracted. A potential step charges about 1 µC on a 3 mm printed electrode (an unverified estimate of 0.2–1 µC, docs/29 A.4), a thousand times the signal at the limit (docs/30 §1).
