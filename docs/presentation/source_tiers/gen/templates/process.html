What went wrong on the way (docs/32 §4): a workflow runs at most two agents at a time on this machine; the first six research agents stopped after about 20 min on the account’s 5-hour usage limit and left only file skeletons; the kit’s first cross-check against the audited model was off by a factor of 2.8 until a loop-plug parameter was read; the session’s worker restarted during the last revision.
