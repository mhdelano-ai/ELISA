On question 2: the model takes the gap of the strip cell as 0.2 mm, which fits 10 µL; the vendor states less than 5 µL, which would mean 0.1 mm or less. On question 4: the vendor’s default full-loop injection draws five loop volumes. On question 6: an in-line filter is a fitting; if a fritted capillary counts as one too, the electrode-capture design becomes the runner-up of tier 1. Choices the orchestrator made and you may overrule are listed in docs/30 §8 as well: gold rather than carbon in tier 1, the read at 200 µL/min, the bead size and operating point of tier 2, one valve as the tier-3 design, the restrictor, the cell gap, and one stability of hydroquinone for the whole liquid path.
