Diamonds are gates. Stage 1 is about 25 bench days with no custom flow hardware, and nothing for a later stage is bought before its gate passes.

Stage 1 measures conjugate sticking on blocked beads, free hydroquinone in the substrate lot, antibody dissociation on beads, the phosphate inhibition constant, contamination, and carryover through the injector. The early gate at about week 5 uses the first two alone, before the kinetics service and the NIST standard are bought. The interdigitated cell and second potentiostat are bought only if Stage 2 shows electrochemical noise is at least 30 percent of the blank variation.
