The audit removed a 25-fold error in conjugate sticking; the review then added uncertainty the model had left out. The ranking of designs never changed.

Chance means the model's probability of a detection limit of 1 pg/mL or better together with a range reaching 1000 pg/mL. The audit's correction moved the result in the favourable direction, on evidence independent of the target: two published anchors agree on the corrected value. The review's corrections moved it back: sampling the stirring factor cost 8 to 12 points, the single-channel default cell 2 to 9 points. Absolute numbers remain uncertain by about a factor of ten either way.
