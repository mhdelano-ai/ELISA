What stands in the way

The blank sets the limit, and the blank is enzyme conjugate sticking where it should not.

Run-to-run variation in conjugate sticking carries nearly all of the blank variance in the model.

Electrochemical noise is at least a thousand times smaller, so the detector barely matters.

The design therefore aims to minimise surface exposed to conjugate per captured IgG.

This held through the audit and the final review. Published flow-injection readouts already detect alkaline phosphatase near 1 fM; every study that analysed its blank traced it to non-specific binding of the conjugate.
