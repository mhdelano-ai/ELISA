Why this design and not the others

FormatOutcomeReason
Beads + stirred in-line cup, dosed by a syringe pumpChosenRecovers simultaneous incubation of sample, beads and conjugate with nothing in the sample vial
Same hardware, sequential stepsFallbackUsed if the detection antibody releases too fast for one-step mode
Fixed packed micro-bedBackupNo bead handling, but more sticking surface per captured IgG
Beads and conjugate in the sample vialRuled outReagent in the sample vial is not allowed; incubation time would also vary along a queue
Merging reagents from a BSM lineRuled outProteins and beads must not pass through the pump
Unstirred holding coilRejectedCapture too slow without stirring: about 6% modelled chance of success
Bead bed on a chip, open capillaryRanked lowerHigher predicted detection limits than the packed bed or the bead formats
The model evaluated 84 combinations of capture format, chemistry, detector and plumbing. The ranking stayed the same through the audit: one-step beads, sequential beads, chip bead bed, packed bed. The in-vial formats led the model but were excluded by the decision that sample vials hold sample only.
