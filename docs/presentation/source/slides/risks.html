Risks and open questions

Beads in rotary valves

Beads cross the selection valve and V2. Rotor wear, lodged beads and carryover are not yet covered by the plan.

Substrate supply

One supplier found for hydroquinone diphosphate, with no purity specification and no second source.

Human IgG contamination

From the operator, the room and the reagents. Barely represented in the model.

Antibody cross-reaction

Both reagents are anti-IgG and are themselves immunoglobulins, so detection may bind capture.

Top of the range

Phosphate inhibition of the enzyme is unverified. At its strong end the upper limit falls to about 460 pg/mL.

Unverified hardware

Pump behaviour at 1 µL/min, sample manager signal wiring, Gamry pairing, and every real device driver.

Awaiting a decision: are shared reagent reservoirs on the instrument acceptable, with nothing in the sample vial?

The bead-in-valve concern was raised after the final review and is the one item on this slide with no experiment or specification attached yet. Options are valves specified for slurries and switched only on bead-free liquid, taking valves out of the bead path with pinch valves, or the packed-bed backup, which has no moving beads.
