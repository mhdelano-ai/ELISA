Success criteria from the project brief. The 100 µL is the injected volume: a full-loop injection with 2× overfill draws about 200 µL. No reagent may be placed in the sample vial.

What stands in the way

The blank sets the limit, and the blank is enzyme conjugate sticking where it should not.

Run-to-run variation in conjugate sticking carries nearly all of the blank variance in the model.

Electrochemical noise is at least a thousand times smaller, so the detector barely matters.

The design therefore aims to minimise surface exposed to conjugate per captured IgG.

This held through the audit and the final review. Published flow-injection readouts already detect alkaline phosphatase near 1 fM; every study that analysed its blank traced it to non-specific binding of the conjugate.

Three external two-position valves (V2 ten-port, V3 and V4 six-port) plus the selection valve on the syringe pump. The sample manager injects sample only, into the cup. The syringe pump doses beads and conjugate from separate reservoirs, then pushes the cup contents through the trap mounted on V2. Beads and conjugate never enter the pump or the sample manager. Open question raised after the review: beads pass through the selection valve and V2, and rotor wear and bead carryover in those valves are not yet addressed in the plan.

Conjugate stuck to a wall: the blank

Capture

2.8 µm streptavidin beads with biotinylated anti-human IgG, Fc-specific. About 500,000 per assay, renewed every run.

Detection

Alkaline phosphatase on an F(ab′)₂ or IgY against the IgG arms, so it binds neither capture antibody nor Fc.

Signal

Hydroquinone diphosphate becomes hydroquinone, which does not foul the carbon electrode.

Buffers and standard

Tris-buffered saline throughout, because phosphate inhibits the enzyme. NISTmAb as standard.

Alkaline phosphatase with hydroquinone diphosphate ranked first among the chemistries because its product does not foul a reused electrode. Two reagent properties decide reachability: how much conjugate sticks to blocked beads and walls, and how much free hydroquinone the substrate lot already contains. Only one supplier of the substrate was found, with no purity specification. The detection target is a half-life on the analyte of an hour or more.

All three use alkaline phosphatase with hydroquinone diphosphate and a single-channel thin-layer cell by default. The fallback and primary share every piece of hardware, so the choice between them is made from the on-bead dissociation measurement in Stage 1, not by a purchase.

Risks and open questions

Beads in rotary valves

Beads cross the selection valve and V2. Rotor wear, lodged beads and carryover are not yet covered by the plan.

Substrate supply

One supplier found for hydroquinone diphosphate, with no purity specification and no second source.

Human IgG contamination

From the operator, the room and the reagents. Barely represented in the model.

Antibody cross-reaction

Both reagents are anti-IgG and are themselves immunoglobulins, so detection may bind capture.

Top of the range

Phosphate inhibition of the enzyme is unverified. At its strong end the upper limit falls to about 460 pg/mL.

Unverified hardware

Pump behaviour at 1 µL/min, sample manager signal wiring, Gamry pairing, and every real device driver.

Awaiting a decision: are shared reagent reservoirs on the instrument acceptable, with nothing in the sample vial?

The bead-in-valve concern was raised after the final review and is the one item on this slide with no experiment or specification attached yet. Options are valves specified for slurries and switched only on bead-free liquid, taking valves out of the bead path with pinch valves, or the packed-bed backup, which has no moving beads.

Diamonds are gates. Stage 1 is about 25 bench days with no custom flow hardware, and nothing for a later stage is bought before its gate passes.

Stage 1 measures conjugate sticking on blocked beads, free hydroquinone in the substrate lot, antibody dissociation on beads, the phosphate inhibition constant, contamination, and carryover through the injector. The early gate at about week 5 uses the first two alone, before the kinetics service and the NIST standard are bought. The interdigitated cell and second potentiostat are bought only if Stage 2 shows electrochemical noise is at least 30 percent of the blank variation.
