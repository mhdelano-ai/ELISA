Blockers

Valves could not close. Channels were cut into the bottom of a 6 mm body; the membrane and plungers were on top.

Electrode pads were unreachable. The die was bonded face-down, the contact pins were in the lid.

Numbers that changed

Read drift: error 0.79 nC against a 0.05 nC target.

Timing: 58.9 min, about 1 min of margin; a 16-run card took 9.5 h.

Pre-electrolysis: about 11 % escape in the tabulated layout, not 1 %.

What held up

Every model figure the reviewer re-ran matched.

17 sources checked: none contradicted, three stretched beyond what they show.

Verdict: A stays the leading concept; wait for gate G1 and the E1.2b result.

Source: docs/15 chip review. The reviewer recomputed the magnet forces, diffusion and electrolysis time constants, charging charge, timing table and carry-over paths with its own scripts, and re-ran a sample of the model estimates on a copy of the repository. Other significant findings: a read window collided with valve and pump activity of the run two places later; the magnet geometry could not gather beads from the far half of the chamber; stranded beads reached a later incubation; A’s carry-over budget was not allocated; a modelling error in the C′ carry-over table flattered C′.
