Sandwich immunoassay for human IgG in buffer. An alkaline-phosphatase label turns a substrate into hydroquinone, which the electrode measures. The success criteria are unchanged from the first generation.

Source: docs/00 project brief and docs/09 chip requirements. The calibration range is about 1–1000 pg/mL. The limit of detection is defined as mean blank plus 3 SD of replicate blanks, read through a calibration curve.
