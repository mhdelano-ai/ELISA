A valve closes when the plunger presses the ceiling onto the floor. No seat ridge, so nothing for beads to catch on.

This stack replaced the first layout, in which the membrane sat 5.8 mm above the channels it was meant to close.

Not to scale. Source: docs/13 Revision B §2.1 and §2.4. Card footprint 75.5 × 50 mm, about 6.2 mm thick. Gates over the magnet slot are limited to 1.5 N so the foil sags by about 15 µm. Open point: there is no endurance evidence for an elastomer membrane on COC; mock-up M4 decides.
