Phase 1 · what the surveys found

Six facts that shaped the candidates

0

precedents for the exact combination

No integrated chip found with this enzyme label and electrochemical readout at 1 pg/mL IgG in 60 min and 100 µL.

Every one

uses a fresh capture surface

All high-sensitivity commercial cartridges found renew the surface each run. Regeneration evidence stops near 50 cycles.

93 % → 20 %

beads trapped, 5 → 20 µL/min

Magnetic trapping in flow is fragile. Dragging beads by magnet into a clean zone is the better-evidenced pattern.

37× → 4×

redox-cycling gain under flow

An interdigitated electrode only pays off when the liquid stands still.

16.5 µL

in one standard chip port

More than the whole path from capture to detector. Through-hole ports hold 0.6 µL.

> 50

valve actuations shown

Published endurance for thermoplastic membrane valves. A cartridge used for a week needs about 1000.

Sources: docs/10 chip literature (checked against abstracts, not full texts) and docs/11 chip fabrication (vendor pages; some gaps marked UNVERIFIED in its §8). Trapping figures are for 4.5 µm beads in a single-magnet chamber. Redox-cycling figure is one carbon interdigitated array at 500 nL/s. The requirements survey (docs/09) added that the capture surface’s own non-specific binding dominates the detection limit, far more than the size of the accumulation volume.
